Your feedback (local tool only)

Tap a feature on the old plate (red), then the same feature in the terrain (blue). Use the slider to see one or the other. Three points fix the position; to check the scale (focal length), mark at least five spread over at least 40° (about one plate), e.g. at both ends of the panorama.

    The terrain is rendered from the S0 DTM20 terrain model with orthophotos draped on it, seen from the solved camera position. Glaciers have changed a lot since the plates were taken: the differences are the point.
