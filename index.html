Svalbard 1900 Phototheodolite stations of the 1906–1928 surveys

Where were the plates taken?

Between 1906 and 1928, Norwegian expeditions mapped Svalbard with a phototheodolite, a levelled survey camera on glass plates. The plates are kept by the Norwegian Polar Institute (series NBR9201), but for most of them we no longer know exactly where the camera stood. This project finds the stations by matching the mountains in the plates to today's terrain model. Every panorama on the map has been checked by eye. Open one to compare the plates with the terrain rendered from the same spot.
